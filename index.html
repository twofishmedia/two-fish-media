Questions

Good questions we hear a lot.

Do you only work with churches?

No. We serve small churches and small nonprofits: food pantries, ministries, community organizations, and similar groups. Nonprofits also get help applying for Google Ad Grants, which offers eligible organizations up to $10,000 a month in free search ads.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your organization's name, and if you ever leave, we hand everything over.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. The monthly plans are month to month.

We already have a website. Can you work with it?

Yes. We move your existing content, photos, and sermons to the new site so nothing gets lost.

How do updates work?

Just email or text us what you need: new service times, an event, this week's sermon. We'll update it, usually within one business day. There are no logins or software for your team to learn.

Can you help with our livestream?

If you already stream on YouTube or Facebook, the Stream plan puts it on your website with a Watch Live page and posts each week's replay for you. To stream worship music, your church will also need a CCLI Streaming License.

Are there any other costs?

Two small ones, paid directly by you, not through us. Your domain name (usually about $10–20 a year) is registered in your organization's name and billed by the registrar. Online giving fees are charged by your giving provider on each gift. We connect the platform you already use at no extra charge, and everything else is included in your plan.
